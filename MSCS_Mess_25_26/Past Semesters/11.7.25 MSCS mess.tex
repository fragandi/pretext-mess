\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black]
    \begin{center}
    \title{{\large \bolder{Women Mathematicians in Quant Finance}}}
    \end{center}
\end{tcolorbox}

If you’re an undergrad student who is curious about how your math skills can be leveraged in different career paths, this session is for you! Join the Carnegie Mellon University Master of Science in Computational Finance (MSCF) program for a thought-provoking webinar that introduces the world of quantitative finance—a field where math, data, and real-world impact intersect. Hear from current female students in the Carnegie Mellon University MSCF program, alongside a successful alumna moderator, as they share why they chose this interdisciplinary field and how it’s opening doors to exciting, high-impact careers. Whether you have heard of quant finance or are just starting to explore your options, this session will help you see how your math skills can lead to opportunities beyond academia. Join the Association of Women in Mathematics for "Shifting the Curve: Women Mathematicians in Quant Finance" on November 13th, 6:00-7:30pm, EST. Registration is required, see poster below for details.

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black]

    \title{{\large \bolder{Problem Solving Group}}}
    \end{center}
\end{tcolorbox}
The Math Problem Solving Group meets weekly to have fun, solve math problems, eat candy, and prepare for upcoming math contests. Meetings are 11:30am-12:30pm, in RNS 207 (the Center for Interdisciplinary Research room). Everyone is welcome, regardless of prior experience in mathematics or math contests.

The first contest will be the Jerry Heuer Team Contest on the morning of Saturday, November 15. It's a fun, accessible contest for teams of up to three students. For more information, or to be added to the problem solving group email list, contact Prof. Wright at wright5@stolaf.edu.
