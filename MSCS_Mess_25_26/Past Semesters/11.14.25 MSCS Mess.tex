There is an excellent article about Jessica Schmidt--math/bio major, SDS/math bio concentrator, class of 2026--in the current issue of the \href{https://wp.stolaf.edu/magazine/}{St. Olaf Magazine}, titled ``The Cipher Protocol." Jessica is a ``master codebreaker" after having been the only individual to have solved all three codebreaking challenges in the annual Kryptos competition. \begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black]
    \begin{center}
    \title{{\large \bolder{CSCI 121 Principles of Computer Science (J-term)
}}}
    \end{center}
\end{tcolorbox}

Dive into computer science with Professor Houssou! This introductory, hands-on course will develop your analytical thinking and problem-solving skills through daily programming exercises. You will apply programming concepts to real-world projects across various fields like science and engineering, as well as music, sociology, finance, art, and more. 

The smaller class size during J-term allows you a more personalized experience into the realm of computer science with one-on-one support from Professor Houssou and close collaboration with your classmates. This is also a great opportunity to build up your technology skills in an ever-increasing technical world.

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black]
    \begin{center}
    \title{{\large \bolder{ CS Unplugged   }}}
    \end{center}
\end{tcolorbox}

This year the CS program is hosting CS Unplugged: a four-part series on internships, jobs, and life as a computer scientist. These sessions will be informal, student-centered, and are a part of a larger goal to get CS students building community within the program. Fill out the form \href{https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScfAnexFhdd-ZAHY0RDX8MpB6A7D7cFtjFYITs-VHnXKzg8XQ/viewform}{at this link} to give your input on what you want to hear at these events!

The first two events will be held on Wednesday, Nov 12th from 3:30-4:30pm, and Wednesday, Nov 19th from 3:30-4:30pm, both in RNS 203. At the end of each event, prizes will be given out! See the poster below for more info.

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black]
    \begin{center}
    \title{{\large \bolder{Problem Solving Group}}}
    \end{center}
\end{tcolorbox}
The Math Problem Solving Group meets weekly to have fun, solve math problems, eat candy, and prepare for upcoming math contests. Meetings are 11:30am-12:30pm, in RNS 207 (the Center for Interdisciplinary Research room). Everyone is welcome, regardless of prior experience in mathematics or math contests.

The first contest will be the Jerry Heuer Team Contest on the morning of Saturday, November 15. It's a fun, accessible contest for teams of up to three students. For more information, or to be added to the problem solving group email list, contact Prof. Wright at wright5@stolaf.edu.
