 \begin{multicols}{2}

 \begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black]
    \begin{center}
    \title{{\large \bolder{MSCS Colloquium 12/8
}}}
    \end{center}
\end{tcolorbox}

Join Charlotte Lambert, a Computer Science faculty candidate, who will be giving an MSCS Department Colloquium talk titled “Fostering Healthier Online Spaces” on Monday, December 8, 2025 at 3:30 PM in RNS 210! Charlotte's research lies in the area of Human-Computer Interaction, particularly in social computing and online governance, with the goal of fostering healthy online spaces through proactive and positive approaches to moderation. In her talk, she will explore novel online moderation strategy that supplement exisiting punitive ones by promoting the "good" part of online communities.
Everyone is welcome to attend!

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black]
    \begin{center}
    \title{{\large \bolder{CSCI 121 Principles of Computer Science (J-term)
}}}
    \end{center}
\end{tcolorbox}

Dive into computer science with Professor Houssou! This introductory, hands-on course will develop your analytical thinking and problem-solving skills through daily programming exercises. You will apply programming concepts to real-world projects across various fields like science and engineering, as well as music, sociology, finance, art, and more. 

The smaller class size during J-term allows you a more personalized experience into the realm of computer science with one-on-one support from Professor Houssou and close collaboration with your classmates. This is also a great opportunity to build up your technology skills in an ever-increasing technical world.

The Math Problem Solving Group meets weekly to have fun, solve math problems, eat candy, and prepare for upcoming math contests. Meetings are 11:30am-12:30pm, in RNS 207 (the Center for Interdisciplinary Research room). Everyone is welcome, regardless of prior experience in mathematics or math contests!
