Happy 2nd full week of classes! We hope you are enjoying your MSCS classes as we get into the swing of things this semester. This Thursday we will be hosting the annual MSCS Showcase, and we hope to see you all there!


 \begin{multicols}{2}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black]
    \begin{center}
    \title{{\large \bolder{MSCS Showcase}}}
    \end{center}
\end{tcolorbox}

Are you a newcomer to MSCS looking to see what we have to offer? Are you an experienced member of MSCS looking to get more involved? Come to the Taylor Swift-inspired  MSCS Showcase on Thursday, September 25 at 4:30 pm in the Buntrock Commons Ballrooms! The MSCS faculty will talk about the many things that make our department special – like classes, research opportunities, and more! \textit{Even if you didn't RSVP by the deadline, please come! We would love to see you there!}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black]
    \begin{center}
    \title{{\large \bolder{Goldwater Scholarship}}}
    \end{center}
\end{tcolorbox}

Sophomores and juniors interested in pursuing a PhD are invited to apply for the Goldwater Scholarship. The Barry M. Goldwater Scholarship Program supports juniors and seniors who intend to pursue a PhD and eventual research career in the sciences or mathematics.  Each year, around 450 students nationwide are awarded one of these prestigious scholarships of \$7500 per year. St. Olaf students have applied successfully for these highly competitive scholarships in the past. If you are interested in applying for this scholarship please look closely at the \href{https://goldwaterscholarship.gov/}{Goldwater web site} and contact your department chair or St. Olaf’s Goldwater campus representative (garrettk@stolaf.edu), for further information. The pre-application deadline is October 21st.

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black]
    \begin{center}
    \title{{\large \bolder{Problem Solving Group}}}
    \end{center}
\end{tcolorbox}
The Math Problem Solving Group meets weekly to have fun, solve math problems, eat candy, and prepare for upcoming math contests. Meetings are 11:30am-12:30pm, in RNS 207 (the Center for Interdisciplinary Research room). Everyone is welcome, regardless of prior experience in mathematics or math contests.

The first contest will be the Jerry Heuer Team Contest on the morning of Saturday, November 15. It's a fun, accessible contest for teams of up to three students. For more information, or to be added to the problem solving group email list, contact Prof. Wright at wright5@stolaf.edu.


\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white, colframe=black]
    \begin{center}
    \title{{\large \bolder{CURI Fall Showcase}}}
    \end{center}
\end{tcolorbox}

On Friday, October 10, 4-6pm, in the Kings Dining Room Complex, the Collaborative Undergraduate Research \& Inquiry (CURI) program will sponsor a poster session that includes all of the summer CURI researchers, Steen Fellows, Magnus the Good Fellows, and academic year researchers. There will be several projects from MSCS and a lot from the sciences in general. Support your friends and peers, and eat some snacks!
